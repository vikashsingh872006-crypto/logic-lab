What is Boolean logic?

Boolean logic is a system of decision-making that uses only two values: 0 (LOW / FALSE) and 1 (HIGH / TRUE). Digital computers use logic gates to process these binary values.

Quick learning points

  • AND gives 1 only when both inputs are 1.
  • OR gives 1 when at least one input is 1.
  • NOT reverses the input.
  • NAND and NOR are the opposites of AND and OR.
  • XOR gives 1 when inputs are different; XNOR when they are the same.
